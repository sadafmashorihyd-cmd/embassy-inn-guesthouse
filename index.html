A guest house built around comfort, cleanliness and security

Tucked just behind Gul Ahmed Ideas on Auto Bhan Road in Latifabad, Embassy Inn Guest House offers spacious AC and Non-AC rooms designed for families, couples and solo travellers passing through Hyderabad. Every room comes with a flat-screen TV, private bathroom, free WiFi and daily housekeeping.

The property runs on standby solar backup so the power never really goes out, and the premises stay covered by CCTV and round-the-clock security — a guest house you can actually relax in.

FAQs

Good to know before you book

What kind of breakfast is served?+

A halal breakfast is available, served in the room on request.

What are the check-in and check-out times?+

Check-in is from 12:00 PM to 12:00 AM, and check-out is between 11:00 AM and 12:00 PM. Early or late check-in can be arranged on request — just message us on WhatsApp.

How much does it cost to stay?+

Rates start from PKR 3,199 for a Non-AC Double Bed Room and go up to PKR 4,700 for an AC Deluxe Room. Full pricing is listed in the Rooms & Rates section above.

Is Embassy Inn Guest House suitable for families?+

Yes — family rooms are available and children of any age are welcome. The property also has 24-hour security and CCTV coverage throughout.

Do couples need to provide any documents?+

Yes, couples are required to present a valid marriage certificate along with original CNIC/NIC at the time of check-in.

What's nearby?+

The guest house sits just behind Gul Ahmed Ideas on Auto Bhan Road, Latifabad, within walking distance of a mosque and close to Hyderabad's main commercial areas.

What payment methods are accepted?+

A 50% advance payment can be sent via bank transfer or Easypaisa to confirm your booking (details shown in the reservation form above). The remaining amount is paid in cash on arrival.
